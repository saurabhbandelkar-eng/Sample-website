Cost questions

Why is pricing per session?

Skin responds differently for everyone. You pay for the session, not a one-size-fits-all package. Sessions are 2–6 weeks apart.

Do packages expire?

Course packages are valid for 3–6 months. Unused sessions can be transferred within the family.

Is a patch test included?

Yes. We patch-test before your first peel or laser session — it is included in the price.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes. Medicines, peels and laser often work together. Your doctor will suggest the right combination — never more than you need.

Do you take insurance?

Cosmetic treatments are self-paid. For medical conditions like eczema, psoriasis or hair loss, we give cash receipts for reimbursement.
