Questions patients ask

Frequently asked

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. Acne usually needs 4–6 peels. Pigmentation takes 4–8 sessions. Laser hair removal needs 6–8 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks. Your doctor gives you a number at the first consultation.

Is it safe for dark Indian skin?

Yes. We choose peels and laser settings for Fitzpatrick IV–V skin — the safest approach for Indian skin tones.

Will it scar?

No. These treatments are designed to heal the skin. When a mole or wart is removed, we take extra care to minimise marks.

Is there any downtime?

Light peels flake for 2–3 days. Medium peels need 3–5 days of heal time. Laser hair removal has no downtime.
