Questions patients ask

Frequently asked

Is a root canal completed in a single sitting without pain?

Yes. Over 90% of routine cases finish in one 45–60 minute sitting. Electronic apex locators and slow-infusion local anaesthesia keep the whole treatment painless.

Do you take emergency walk-ins?

Yes. We keep same-day slots for throbbing toothache, abscess, broken crowns and dental trauma. Call or message us by morning and we will fit you in that day.

Why a specialist clinic instead of a general dentist?

Complex work — implants, root canals, veneers — is done by MDS postgraduate specialists in each discipline, not general dentists. You get hospital-level specialist care.

How long do implants take?

Usually 3–4 months and 2–3 visits. We confirm the timeline and a written cost before anything starts.
