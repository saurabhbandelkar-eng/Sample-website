Questions patients ask

Frequently asked

How long until I can return to work?

Most back pain patients return to desk work in 2–4 weeks with 6–8 sessions. Surgery rehab takes longer — we give you a week-by-week target.

Do you come home?

Yes. We do home visits 7 days a week in Andheri and nearby areas. Same physiotherapist, same treatment — we bring the equipment.

How long is one session?

45–60 minutes. The first assessment is 45 minutes. Home visits are 45–60 minutes depending on your condition.

Is physiotherapy painful?

Some stretching is uncomfortable — that is normal. We work within your pain limit and explain every step. Pain should never be sharp.
