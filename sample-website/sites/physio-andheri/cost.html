Cost questions

Should I take sessions or a package?

Packages save 12–18% and keep you consistent. If your condition is short-term, pay per session — no pressure to buy a package.

Are there extra charges for home visits?

No. Travel is included within 5 km of Andheri. Beyond that, we confirm a small travel fee before booking.

What about cancellation?

Free cancellation up to 2 hours before your session. You can reschedule by phone or WhatsApp.

Can I claim insurance?

We give cash receipts for all sessions. Most insurers reimburse physiotherapy under outpatient cover — check with your insurer.

Is the first assessment required?

Yes. We assess before treating — it keeps your treatment safe and stops you paying for sessions that do not fit your condition.
