About the doctor

Meet Dr. Rajesh Menon

Founder & Lead Physiotherapist at Andheri Physio Care, a Physiotherapy centre in Andheri East, Mumbai.

Dr. Rajesh Menon is a certified sports physiotherapist with 10+ years of experience in musculoskeletal rehabilitation, holding an MPT in Musculoskeletal Sciences. The clinic was built around one rule: every session is one-to-one with the same physiotherapist — no rotating assistants, no rushed 15-minute slots.

Every patient starts with a first assessment: movement tests, posture checks and a written rehab plan with week-by-week targets. Home exercises are written down so the plan works between visits too.

The approach is simple: explain everything first, show the cost before treatment, and keep sessions unhurried. Clinical excellence and patient comfort co-exist — they are not trade offs.

“We believe rehabilitation should be one-to-one, explained and written down. Every plan shows week-by-week targets and the total cost — before anything starts.”
